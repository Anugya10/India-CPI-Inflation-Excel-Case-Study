# India CPI Inflation Analysis (2013 - May 2023)

An Excel data analytics case study on India's Consumer Price Index (CPI). Acting as an analyst at the National Statistical Office, I analysed monthly CPI data to find key inflation trends and what drove them.

Built as part of the Data Analytics course at Coding Ninjas.

## Dataset

- **Source:** CPI index data published by the Government of India (via the Coding Ninjas course platform)
- **Period:** January 2013 to May 2023 (monthly)
- **Sectors:** Rural, Urban, Rural+Urban
- **Categories:** about 27 categories plus the General Index (base year 2012 = 100)

## Questions and insights

### Q1. Category contribution to the CPI basket (May 2023)
- **Pan, tobacco and intoxicants has the highest contribution: 9.31%** (Rural 10.0%, Urban 9.5%).
- Its index is about 201, the highest of all 12 categories.
- Other categories range from 7.6% (Transport and communication) to 8.6% (Clothing and footwear).

### Q2. Year-on-year inflation trend (2017 - 2023)
- **2022 had the highest inflation: 6.62%**, with monthly YoY peaking at 7.79% in April 2022.
- Inflation was moderate in 2017-2019 (3.3% to 4.0%) and elevated in 2020-2022 (5.8% to 6.6%).
- Jan-May 2023 inflation is still about 5.6% against the same months of 2022.
- Reason for 2022: the Russia-Ukraine war raised crude oil, edible oil, wheat and fertiliser prices, on top of lingering COVID supply-chain disruption.

### Q3. Food inflation (June 2022 - May 2023)
- **Highest month-on-month food inflation: October 2022 (+1.01%). Lowest: December 2022 (-1.35%).**
- **Spices** contributed the most to food inflation (+33.1 index points), followed by Cereals (+19.6) and Milk (+14.6).
- **Oils and fats** fell the most (-32.4), then Vegetables (-13.9).

### Q4. Impact of COVID-19 (onset March 2020)
- Average inflation rose from **4.51% to 5.67%** in the 12 months after March 2020 (+1.16 pp).
- Biggest rises: Oils and fats (+10.6 pp), Pulses (+7.3 pp), Transport and communication (+5.9 pp), Meat and fish (+5.2 pp).
- Vegetables fell (-12.4 pp) and Health fell (-2.1 pp).

### Q5. Imported oil prices and inflation (2021 - 2023)
- **Meat and fish (r = 0.40) and Oils and fats (r = 0.38)** show the strongest correlation with oil price changes.
- Fuel and light (0.13) and Transport and communication (0.08) barely move with oil.
